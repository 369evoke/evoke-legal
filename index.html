Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account: your email address and, if you choose to add them, your birthdate and full name (for Numerology), journal and manifestation entries, check-ins, and any photos you upload to your Vision Board.

How we use it

To run the app: syncing your entries and progress across your devices, sending the reminders you set, and processing your subscription. We don't sell your personal data.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

Your data, your control

You can export everything from Settings → Export my data. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below. We'll confirm deletion within 30 days.

Your rights for EU/UK users (GDPR)

If you're in the EU or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us below to exercise these rights.

Your rights for California users (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Contact us below to make a request.

Children's privacy

Evoke is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us below and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: 369evoke@gmail.com
